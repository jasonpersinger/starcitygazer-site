City Manager Report on Gilmer Traffic Calming Project

The City Manager reported on the Gilmer Traffic Calming Project, noting that staff worked with community volunteers and local artists to prepare five sites and complete the first of five street murals honoring the Star City Medical Society and Black medical professionals from the Jim Crow era.

Section 7, d.

Gilmer Traffic Calming Project Staff recently joined community volunteers and local artists for the Gilmer Traffic Calming Project, preparing five sites and completing the first of five planned street murals. The first mural honors the Star City Medical Society and the legacy of Black medical professionals who served Roanoke during the Jim Crow era, and the project has already brought residents together to talk about both the history and future of Gilmer Avenue.

Public Matters Follow-Up on Police Actions Regarding Trespassing, Encampments, and Food Truck

Staff reported on Roanoke Police Department follow-ups from the August 17 meeting: an Elm Avenue property was enrolled in the Trespass Bar Program with adjacent lot cleanup; campsites near 14th Street SE were notified to vacate and cleaned up; and an inquiry regarding a food truck found the operator has proper permits with no enforceable violations.

Section 7, d.

The Roanoke Police Department followed up with three residents who spoke on separate matters at the August 17 meeting. One resident spoke about trespassing and misconduct happening on their property on Elm Avenue. RPD has reached out to have the property enrolled in the Trespass Bar Program and Code Enforcement has cited the owner of an adjacent lot. As a result, the lot has been mowed and cleaned up resulting in less gathering and loitering. The Police Department will continue to monitor this area and address any future issues as needed. Another resident expressed concern about encampments in the area of 14th Street SE. In collaboration with Code Enforcement, the campsites located on this

The Police Department will continue to work within the applicable City Code to address and clean up these campsites. A third resident expressed concern about the operation of a food truck. RPD reports that the food truck owner has the proper permits to operate within the City. The Police Department found no legally enforceable violations related to this complaint.

Public Matters Follow-Up on Wilmont Park Area Curbing and Stormwater Runoff

Staff followed up on a resident's inquiry regarding curbing and stormwater runoff in the Wilmont Park area. Stormwater staff determined the runoff is caused by the private driveway's grade and that curb and gutter installation would only be aesthetic rather than solve the problem, offering the owner options for handling the driveway runoff.

  • Utilities
  • Transportation

Section 7, d.

Wilmont Park Area Curbing At the September 8 meeting, a resident inquired about action on a longstanding request for curbing and stormwater runoff issues. The Engineering division reports that this project’s priority listing was #214 in 2000 and it is currently listed as #198. The City Stormwater team evaluated the property and discussed the runoff concern with the resident. They discovered that the water runoff the resident is experiencing results from the grade of the driveway on the property. Curb and gutter installation would not fix the runoff problem; it would only be aesthetic. Stormwater offered the property owner options for addressing the driveway water runoff.
