Amendments to Chapter 36.2 Zoning Code for Housing Development and Land Use

Following the continuation of a public hearing, the City Council adopted an ordinance and a resolution approving proposed amendments to Chapter 36.2 (Zoning) of the Code of the City of Roanoke. The amendments are intended to facilitate context-sensitive housing development, promote community health and wellness, encourage purposeful land use, update use tables for multiple purpose, industrial, and planned unit development districts, and clarify and correct zoning code elements.

Section 2, 1.

Request of the City of Roanoke to propose amendments to Chapter 36.2, Zoning, of the Code of the City of Roanoke (1979), as amended, intended to facilitate context-sensitive housing development; to promote community health and wellness; to encourage purposeful land use to make changes to the use tables for multiple purpose, industrial, and planned unit development districts; and to make changes to clarify and correct elements of the zoning code, such amendments being for the public purposes of promoting public health, public safety, and good zoning practice. Katharine Gray, Interim Planning Manager; Spokesperson. Adopted Ordinance No. 43434-070626. (7-0) Adopted Resolution No. 43435-070626. (7-0)
