2. PRESENTATIONS AND ACKNOWLEDGEMENTS

Mayor Cobb advised that, going forward, the 2:00 pm meeting would begin with the Council Members providing "shout-outs" or announcing community events recently attended. The Council Members made the following announcements:

Council Member Hagen “shouted out” the Living Legends Luncheon held on Thursday, July 30, at Melrose Plaza honoring retired pastors Edward Burton and William Lee, musician Durell Coleman, retired Gainsboro Library director Carla Lewis, and Roanoke Tribune publisher Stan Hale.

Council Member Sanchez-Jones "shouted-out" Derek Ruben and the D&G Wonderland Team, who hosted a community event on Saturday, August 1, at McCadden Park.

Vice-Mayor McGuire "shouted-out" the emergency responders and Fire-EMS staff that responded to a head-on bicycle accident that recently occurred on the greenway.

Council Member Nash "shouted out" the summer graduates of Roanoke City Public Schools and wished them the best in their future endeavors.

Council Member Volosin "shouted-out" all poll workers and the City Registrar for all their hard work in preparation of Election Day which is Tuesday, August 4, and announced that Valley Metro buses are fare free that day.

Mayor Cobb "shouted-out" the Talent in the Star Show hosted by Total Action for Progress (TAP) and noted that it was the last show of that kind; Greg Addison's Fashion/Hair Show "Emerald City", which was a phenomenal show; and the City of Roanoke as a Top Employer for Interns for the past four years. Proclamation declaring Saturday, August 8, 2026, as the Commonwealth Day of Play. Mayor Cobb presented the proclamation to Valerie Brown, Executive Director, Greater Williamson Road Area Business Association; and Liz Sturgill and William Dixon, board members in attendance.

The City Manager offered the following comments:

Preparations are well underway for the upcoming youth sports season. Youth soccer registration is strong, with 59 teams registered across multiple age divisions, and the season is scheduled to kick off on August 22, providing recreational opportunities for hundreds of young athletes throughout the community.

Interest in youth flag football has also been impressive, with 125 participants currently registered—enough to form approximately 11 to 13 teams, including two girls' teams in the 13U division. The flag football season is expected to begin the week of August 24.

Installation of new security doors in the Municipal North Lobby will begin on August 4 as part of ongoing efforts to enhance building safety and security. To minimize disruptions to daily operations, the contractor will perform the work overnight. Once installation is complete, employees will access the secured areas using their City identification badge, while visitors will require an employee escort. The doors will automatically release during an emergency to ensure safe and unrestricted egress from the building.
