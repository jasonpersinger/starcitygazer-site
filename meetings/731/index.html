City of Roanoke, Virginia

CITY COUNCIL

October 19, 2026 2:00 PM

City Council Chamber 215 Church Avenue, SW

AGENDA

The City of Roanoke is a safe, caring and economically vibrant community in which to live, learn, work, play and prosper. A vibrant urban center with strong neighborhoods set amongst the spectacular beauty of Virginia's Blue Ridge.

NOTICE:

City Council meetings will be televised live and replayed on RVTV Channel 3 on Thursdays at 7:00 pm, and Saturdays from 10:00 am to 5:00 pm; and video streamed through YouTube Live at youtube.com/RoanokeVa. Council meetings are offered with closed captioning for the deaf or hard of hearing.

1. CALL TO ORDER - ROLL CALL

All present. The Invocation was delivered by The Reverend Alexander MacPhail, Rector, Christ Episcopal Church. The Pledge of Allegiance to the Flag of the United States of America was led by Mayor Joseph L. Cobb. Welcome

ANNOUNCEMENTS:

2. PRESENTATIONS AND ACKNOWLEDGEMENTS

A proclamation declaring October 25 - 31, 2026 as National Lead Poisoning Prevention Week. Mayor Cobb read and presented the proclamation to Desi Wynter, Housing Programs and Policy Manager, and Katie Kennedy, Lead Safe Roanoke Program Manager and staff in attendance. The Mayor advised that going forward, the 2:00 pm meeting would begin with the Council Members providing "shout-outs" or announcing community events recently attended. The Council Members made the following announcements:

Council Member Nash “shouted out” the Excel Center located in Melrose Plaza and commented that they hosted a meeting to recruit more adult learners seeking high school diplomas.

Council Member Volosin “shouted out” Senator Warner and Feeding Southwest VA and asked everyone to make a donation to the food pantry.

Council Member Powers "shouted-out" the Melrose Branch Library Fall Festival and the Fire-EMS department who visited local schools to educate them on fire safety.

Council Member Hagen “shouted out” the Bear Theatre production of Death by Design, a murder mystery comedy written by local playwright Stacy Upton.

Council Member Sanchez-Jones "shouted out" GO Fest organizers and the Champs Boxing Gym for a successful event this past weekend.

Vice-Mayor McGuire "shouted out" the GO Fest organizers, thanked poll workers for their hard work and advised that early voting has begun.

Mayor Cobb "shouted out" the Blue Ridge Literacy annual celebration and encouraged everyone to get the booklet produced titled "Writing from the Heart."

PUBLIC MATTERS FOLLOW-UP Carol Corbin, Communications Manager, reported on the following matters from the September 21 and October 5, 2026, Meetings:

Bus Rider At the September 21 meeting, a resident inquired about a direct route on the 15 bus to the Valley View Walmart. The resident also reported her concern to the GRTC Board, which operates the Valley Metro bus system. Valley Metro officials are looking into the possibility of future route modifications and will follow up with the resident.

In the meantime, Valley Metro confirmed that the resident can take any bus to the transfer station, and get on the 11 bus, for drop off at Walmart. Passengers with a qualifying disability can also contact Valley Metro for more information about paratransit services.

Meals Tax A resident spoke at the September 21 meeting with questions about City meals tax revenue and deferred maintenance projects. The August 2026 Monthly Financial Report, along with a breakdown of completed and in-progress City HVAC projects and elevator modernization work, were provided to the resident by the City’s FOIA Officer.

Safe Streets for All Also at the September 21 meeting, a representative from a local organization brought concerns about the age of the City’s Street Design Manual. Public Works reports that it is in the process of updating its published manual. However, the department is following design practices that are based primarily on the latest applicable federal and state standards and supplemented by recognized industry guidance such as the National Association of City Transportation Officials.
