2. PRESENTATIONS AND ACKNOWLEDGEMENTS

Mayor Cobb advised that, going forward, the 2:00 pm meeting would begin with the Council Members providing "shout-outs" or announcing community events recently attended. The Council Members made the following announcements:

Council Member Powers “shouted out” the ribbon cuttings for Urban Commercial Cleaning, LLC, and Spice Titan and expressed excitement about the new businesses in the City of Roanoke.

COMMENTS OF THE CITY MANAGER.

The City Manager shared the following comments:

I’d like to recognize our Parks and Recreation Department, which recently received three awards at the Virginia Recreation and Park Society’s annual conference in Richmond. The department was recognized for Washington Park Pool as Best New Facility, the Goat Grazing Initiative for Best New Environmental Sustainability, and its creative “Do You Gnome Your Park and Rec Professionals?” campaign for Most Creative Marketing Strategy. Congratulations to everyone involved on this well-deserved recognition.

I’d also like to recognize the City employees who participated in our annual Snowfighter training on October 23. Staff from several departments took part in classroom instruction and hands-on exercises to prepare for winter weather, including practicing snow removal techniques and operating large equipment. This training helps ensure our crews are ready to respond safely and efficiently to snow and ice, keeping our roads clear and helping residents get where they need to go throughout the winter season.

I would like to take a moment to remind all City employees that Benefits Open Enrollment for the 2027 health plan year is currently underway and will close on Tuesday, November 10th. All benefit-eligible employees must complete their enrollment, even if they plan to keep their current benefits or waive coverage. If you do not make your elections by the deadline, your current benefits will end on December 31, 2026.

I’d also like to take a moment to remember Jeff White, our Code Enforcement Officer, who passed away on October 18. Jeff was a valued member of our organization and a respected leader who made a meaningful contribution to our team and the community. He will be greatly missed by his colleagues and all who had the opportunity to work with him. Our thoughts and condolences are with his family, friends, and coworkers during this difficult time.
