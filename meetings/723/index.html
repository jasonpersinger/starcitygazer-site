COMMENTS OF THE CITY MANAGER.

The City Manager shared the following comments:

Juneteenth The Roanoke Cultural Collective will host its annual Juneteenth Family Reunion at Eureka Park on Saturday, June 20, 2026. This free community festival brings families and neighbors together for a day of celebration, connection and remembrance. Activities include live music, soul food, children’s zone, local vendor marketplace and programs designed for all generations. The event honors the African American families whose contribution help shape northwest Roanoke and continues the tradition of community gathering and cultural celebration. Throughout the day, faith leaders, civic organizations and local musicians will help lead the festivities and share in the spirit of Juneteenth. Everyone is welcome to attend and enjoy this day of heritage, fellowship and community pride.

Freedom Festival Celebrating 250 years of liberty, this will be a large-scale community event designed to celebrate the nation’s 250th anniversary with family-friendly programming including live music, food vendors, recreational activities, and educational engagement. The event will conclude with a fifteen-to-twenty-minute professional fireworks display provided by Master Displays as the signature finale. The program is being coordinated with community partners, including Five Points Music Foundation for live entertainment and Roanoke Public Libraries for interactive literacy and history-based activities. Along with a wide range of free family attractions and community engagement features throughout the event. The Roanoke Police Department will also be onsite supporting public safety and community engagement activities. Overall, the Freedom Festival is designed as an inclusive city-wide celebration that brings together entertainment, education, and community connection.

Virginia Sunbucks Program Launch for 2026, providing a one-time $120.00 summer food benefit to eligible school-age children. Eligibility includes children in foster care, or those enrolled in SNAP, TANF, income-based Medicaid or free and reduced-priced meal programs. Most eligible participants will be automatically enrolled with additional application options available online or by phone or via paper forms. Outreach efforts are underway to ensure community awareness.
