AGENDA

The City of Roanoke is a safe, caring and economically vibrant community in which to live, learn, work, play and prosper. A vibrant urban center with strong neighborhoods set amongst the spectacular beauty of Virginia's Blue Ridge.

NOTICE:

City Council meetings will be televised live and replayed on RVTV Channel 3 on Thursdays at 7:00 pm, and Saturdays from 10:00 am to 5:00 pm; and video streamed through YouTube Live at youtube.com/RoanokeVa. Council meetings are offered with closed captioning for the deaf or hard of hearing.

The City Manager offered the following comments:

The City’s annual external agency grant program is now open and accepting applications through December 16. The program provides funding opportunities for organizations whose projects and services support City Council’s strategic initiatives. To help prospective applicants, the City will offer two optional training sessions on November 23 and December 3. These sessions will cover program requirements, walk through the application process and provide an opportunity to ask questions. For more information, including application details, visit the City’s website.

Construction continues on the In-River Park, with a major portion of the new ADA-accessible river access ramp completed on November 11. The ramp is located beneath the Wasena pedestrian bridge, between Wasena and Vic Thomas parks. Crews are scheduled to resume work today, Monday, November 16. The pedestrian bridge will be closed while crews complete this phase of construction. Once complete, the ramp will make it easier for people of all abilities to safely access and enjoy the Roanoke River, while providing more opportunities for residents to connect with the river.

The Wasena Bridge ribbon-cutting will be held on November 30, at 8:30 a.m. PUBLIC MATTERS FOLLOW-UP Carol Corbin, Community Engagement Manager, reported on the following matters from the October 19 and November 2, 2026, meetings:

Williamson Road Bike Lanes On October 19, a resident shared concerns about the lack of mobility lane striping on newly repaved Williamson Road. The Transportation Division followed up with the resident to inform them that work to stripe the recently paved road was in progress and has not yet been completed. Once completed, the mobility lane striping on Williamson Road will extend from Pocohontas Avenue NE to Oaklawn Avenue NW and then pick back up north of Hershberger Road from Airport Road to the city/county line. Although the Hershberger Road intersection will not be striped for mobility lanes, we are in the process of exploring alternative low-traffic neighborhood routes to help micromobility users safely access the area. A draft map of the neighborhood route in consideration was provided to the resident.

ADA Sewer Concern Also at the October 19 meeting, a resident expressed concern that the Western Virginia Water Authority’s policy around sewer lines may create a barrier to adding Accessory Dwelling Units to properties. This issue falls under the authority of the Western Virginia Water Authority Board. City Manager Turner plans to raise the matter at the board’s next meeting on November 19. The City will provide the resident with additional information following that discussion.
