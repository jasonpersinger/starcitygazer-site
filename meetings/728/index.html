All present. Mayor Cobb asked for a Moment of Silence in observance of the 25th anniversary of the September 11, 2001, terrorist attack on the United States of America. 2. PRESENTATIONS AND ACKNOWLEDGEMENTS

Mayor Cobb advised that, going forward, the 2:00 pm meeting would begin with the Council Members providing "shout-outs" or announcing community events recently attended. The Council Members made the following announcements:

Council Member Powers “shouted out” the Friends of Mountain View and the fundraising event to be hosted every first Thursday of the month featuring music acts, etc, to raise money for needed repairs to the Fishburn home.

Council Member Volosin “shouted out” America Works and the local labor union who put on the Labor Day Parade in downtown Roanoke.

Council Member Nash "shouted-out" the Festival of India, which was a great success, and all the festivals across the City that celebrate the culture of Roanoke.

Council Member Sanchez-Jones “shouted out” the Noble Neighborhood Association for their great event and the organizers of the Labor Day Parade held in downtown Roanoke on Saturday, September 5.

Council Member Hagen "shouted out" Mayor Cobb on the State of the City Address and commended him for giving a speech at 7:30 am and having a sold-out event. He also acknowledged the Bear Theater for wrapping up another great season.

Mayor Cobb “shouted out” Overdose Awareness Day and noted that it was the third year he presented a Proclamation at the Taubman Museum of Art. He noted that the event was an opportunity for the community to come together in remembrance of those who died from an overdose.

Presentation by the Virginia Tech Foundation on the RoVA Labs facility. Elizabeth McClanahan, Chief Executive Officer, Virginia Tech Foundation, Inc., and President, Virginia Tech Corporate Research Center, Inc., presented a reimbursement check to the City of Roanoke and advised that the partnership has been very meaningful and impactful.

COMMENTS OF THE CITY MANAGER.

The City Manager shared the following comments:

On September 2 and 3, I had the opportunity to bring our department directors together for a two-day leadership retreat at RoVa Labs. It was a great opportunity to step away from our day-to-day work, strengthen our leadership as a team, share perspectives, and focus on how we can continue working together to deliver the best possible services to our community.

The Wasena Bridge Replacement project is now approximately 95% complete, with the contractor focused on the final bridge and roadway improvements. We’re excited to see this project nearing completion and look forward to reopening the bridge and reconnecting the Wasena and Old Southwest neighborhoods in the coming weeks.

September is National Preparedness Month, and our Emergency Management and Stormwater teams are working together to help residents be ready for emergencies and severe weather. Throughout the month, staff will be sharing preparedness tips and connecting with the community to encourage everyone to sign up for emergency alerts, understand their risks, build an emergency kit, and have a plan.
