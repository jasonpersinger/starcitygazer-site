City Manager Comments on Leadership Retreat, Wasena Bridge, and Emergency Preparedness

The City Manager provided updates reporting on a department directors' leadership retreat held at RoVa Labs on September 2-3, progress on the Wasena Bridge Replacement project (approximately 95% complete with reopening expected in the coming weeks), and activities for National Preparedness Month involving Emergency Management and Stormwater teams.

  • Transportation
  • Public safety
  • Council & government
Read supporting evidence

Section 7, g.

COMMENTS OF THE CITY MANAGER. The City Manager shared the following comments: On September 2 and 3, I had the opportunity to bring our department directors together for a two-day leadership retreat at RoVa Labs.

Section 7, g.

The Wasena Bridge Replacement project is now approximately 95% complete, with the contractor focused on the final bridge and roadway improvements. We’re excited to see this project nearing completion and look forward to reopening the bridge and reconnecting the Wasena and Old Southwest neighborhoods in the coming weeks. September is National Preparedness Month, and our Emergency Management and Stormwater teams are working together to help residents be ready for emergencies and severe weather.

COMMENTS OF THE CITY MANAGER.

The City Manager shared the following comments:

On September 2 and 3, I had the opportunity to bring our department directors together for a two-day leadership retreat at RoVa Labs. It was a great opportunity to step away from our day-to-day work, strengthen our leadership as a team, share perspectives, and focus on how we can continue working together to deliver the best possible services to our community.

The Wasena Bridge Replacement project is now approximately 95% complete, with the contractor focused on the final bridge and roadway improvements. We’re excited to see this project nearing completion and look forward to reopening the bridge and reconnecting the Wasena and Old Southwest neighborhoods in the coming weeks.

September is National Preparedness Month, and our Emergency Management and Stormwater teams are working together to help residents be ready for emergencies and severe weather. Throughout the month, staff will be sharing preparedness tips and connecting with the community to encourage everyone to sign up for emergency alerts, understand their risks, build an emergency kit, and have a plan.
