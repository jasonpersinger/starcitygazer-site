COMMENTS OF THE CITY MANAGER.

The City Manager offered the following comments:

All-America City Award I am excited to officially announce that Roanoke is a nine-time All-America City! The All-America City Award consists of a written application and an in-person presentation focused on three distinctive projects relating to strengthening civic health and building trust. The three projects we highlighted this year were Roanoke's Homelessness Response, the Grant-Ready Roanoke program, and Arts Connect Neighbors. A delegation of seven City staff and community members represented Roanoke at the competition. Community Engagement Manager Adam Fajardo led the effort. Also, he was joined by Council Member Sanchez-Jones, Grant Manager Helen Ferguson, Human Services Administrator Matthew Crookshank, and Police Officer Jacob Underwood. Additionally, Renea Taylor, Founder of FedUP, and Robyn Mitchell, Arts Connect Catalog Artist joined the delegation. Twenty finalist cities from across the country competed in this year's All-America City Award and there were ten winners. Additionally, the All-America City Director noted that this year's competition was one of the fiercest she's seen in the eight years of the organization's history. About half of the cities sent delegations of over 20 people, with the largest having over 50 people. Compared to more elaborate presentations, Roanoke shined through authentic storytelling and a collaborative art project that involved other All-America cities. Our three programs earned national recognition because the panel of judges saw that Roanoke is strengthening our community and building trust with the people we serve.

Water Matters to Roanoke Program The Office of Communications and Engagement will kick off the "Water Matters to Roanoke" Water Conservation Campaign in coordination with the Western Virginia Water Authority. The campaign will raise awareness about Roanoke's water resources and the conditions under which the Water Authority may implement water restrictions. We are going to educate residents and encourage water use, align ourselves with water waste content and explain the Drought Contingency Plan, to prepare Roanoke's for a seamless transition to voluntary and mandatory restriction campaigns, if implemented. Beginning today, the Office of Community Engagement will amplify its useful, clear and actionable content, including weekly water level updates and water conservation tips across City social media platforms, website homepage banners, RVTV Channel 3, internal and external newsletters, and local news. In the event that the Western Virginia Water Authority implements a drought contingency plan, Community Engagement will initiate amplification of the Authority's restriction campaign.
