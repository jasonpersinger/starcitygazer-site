COMMENTS OF THE CITY MANAGER.

The City Manager shared the following comments:

Last week, the Roanoke City Police Department held a Love Where You Live event at Ferncliff Apartments, bringing together officers, City staff, volunteers, and community partners. This was a great example of how departments come together as a team and the importance of communication and collaboration as we work to improve neighborhoods, connect residents with resources, and support family safety, empowerment, and student success.

Our Stormwater team recently completed the City’s annual report documenting our compliance with our stormwater permit. The report is an important communication tool that helps us share the work we’re doing to protect our local waterways, including 10 citywide street-sweeping cycles that removed more than 1,000 tons of sediment, cleaning storm drains, inspecting nearly 29,000 feet of pipe, and reaching more than 38,000 residents through public outreach. I want to recognize the team for not only doing this important work, but also communicating that work to our community.

Several members of our Parks and Recreation leadership team recently attended the National Recreation and Parks Association Conference in Philadelphia. It’s important that our leadership team stays connected to what’s happening nationally, learns from other communities, and brings those ideas back to Roanoke. That kind of collaboration and willingness to learn from one another is what makes a strong team players and helps us continue to improve the programs and services we provide to our residents.
