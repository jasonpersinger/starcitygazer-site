City Manager Report on Quiet Pickleball Implementation at Four Parks

The City Manager reported that park maintenance staff installed pickleball signs and equipment boxes at Shrine Hill, Belmont, Raleigh Court, and Eureka parks. Starting September 15, players at those locations must use quieter pickleballs provided by the City to reduce noise for neighbors.
